Synergy Strategy

3. Content Tailoring vs. Form Autofills

  • Form Autofills (e.g. Simplify): Automates portal text fields (name, phone, demographics, work histories).
  • Content Tailoring (job-search): Customizes experience bullets to match JD keywords, builds cover letters, compiles PDFs.
  • The Perfect Integration: Our tool structures the content and compiles customized resumes -> Autofill extensions load and submit.
