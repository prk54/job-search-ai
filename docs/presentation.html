Job Market Reality

2. Why Tailored Resumes Matter Today

The ATS Gatekeeper

Over 98% of large companies use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS). If your resume does not match the exact keywords of the job description, it gets automatically filtered out before a human recruiter ever sees it.

The 6-Second Recruiter Screen

Recruiters spend an average of just 6 seconds scanning a resume. If they don't see the exact matching skills and highlights in your top three bullet points, they move to the next candidate.

Hyper-Competitive Job Market

Easy-apply buttons mean recruiters receive thousands of applications. A generic, one-size-fits-all resume cannot compete with a resume that is tailored specifically for the role.

The Gold Standard

Tailoring is no longer optional. To get callbacks, your resume must match the target job description with 90%+ keyword alignment.
